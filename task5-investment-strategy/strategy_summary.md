# Week 5 — Investment Strategy Summary

**Research cut-off: 2 November 2026**

## Strategic choice
A balanced, quality-led automotive-sector satellite, diversified across passenger/utility vehicles, two-wheelers, premium motorcycles, commercial vehicles, components/technology and batteries. It is not intended to replace a diversified equity core.

## Market opportunity and context
SIAM's Q2 FY2026–27 release reported domestic passenger-vehicle sales of 1.36 million units (+30.7% y/y), utility vehicles at 68% of PV sales (+34.2%), passenger-car sales +24.9%, two-wheelers +15.2%, three-wheelers +23.6%, and commercial vehicles +28.7%. PV exports declined 5.2%, with SIAM citing a higher base and West Asia logistics constraints. Growth rates should not be extrapolated mechanically.

Maruti Suzuki reported record Q2 FY27 total sales of 696,654 (+26.5%), domestic PV market share of 40.8% (+2.2 percentage points), about 17 days of network inventory and a pending booking backlog near 200,000. The same release reported EBIT down year-on-year amid West-Asia-linked commodity inflation: strong volumes do not automatically translate into margin growth.

PM E-DRIVE's outlay is ₹11,900 crore and implementation is extended to 31 March 2028 for supported categories, with category-specific exceptions. New CAFE norms apply from 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2032 and tighten the benchmark by about 16.7% across the period. IEA reported India's electric-car sales rose 75% in 2025 to 165,000, nearly 4% of total car sales.

## Entry and governance
1. Tranche 1: 40% of intended position after governance, liquidity, latest financial disclosure and valuation checks; seek 10–15% margin of safety to conservative fair value for cyclical names.
2. Tranche 2: 30% after quarterly results/monthly sales confirm volume quality, mix, margin and cash conversion.
3. Tranche 3: 30% only after price/volume structure stabilises and the thesis remains intact. No automatic averaging down.

Review the sleeve if it falls 15% from its high-water mark; at 20%, require a formal hold/reduce/rebuild decision and updated bear-case valuation. Rebalance quarterly or semi-annually, not on every daily price move. No leverage or margin borrowing is assumed.

## Key risks and mitigations
Commodity/energy shocks; geopolitical/export disruption; EV economics and execution; CAFE/safety compliance costs; financing and affordability; supplier concentration; quality/recall; foreign exchange; cyber risk; inventory and valuation risk. The report maps these to monitoring indicators, portfolio actions and thesis-break rules.
