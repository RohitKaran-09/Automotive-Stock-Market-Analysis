**Research cut-off:** 2 November 2026

Integrate Weeks 1–4 into a disciplined, illustrative three-year strategy for India's listed automotive sector. This project combines industry demand and policy context, M&M/Maruti fundamental findings, historical technical research, and the prior risk/opportunity assessment.

## Strategy at a glance
The proposal is a quality-led, diversified **automotive-sector satellite**, not a complete household portfolio or a personalised recommendation. The illustrative sleeve allocates 90% across nine OEM/component/battery exposures and 10% to cash/liquid reserve. Individual positions begin at no more than 15%. Entry is staged 40% / 30% / 30% and gated by updated disclosures, valuation margin of safety, operating evidence and technical stabilisation.

Three-year bear/base/bull results are sensitivity illustrations, not target returns or probability-weighted forecasts. They do not model individual company earnings paths, tax, fees, execution costs or active rebalancing.

## Current evidence
The report uses SIAM Q2 FY2026–27 sales data (15 October 2026), Maruti Suzuki's Q2/H1 FY2026–27 update, PM E-DRIVE scheme information, the Government's CAFE framework effective April 2027, IEA Global EV Outlook 2026, GST 2.0 policy context and dated supply-chain/competition reporting. It explicitly notes that strong volumes do not guarantee margin expansion and that the October 12 technical snapshot from the previous task is historical, not a current trading signal.
