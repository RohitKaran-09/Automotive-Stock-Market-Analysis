# Week 4 — Risk and Opportunity Assessment (Summary)

**Research cut-off:** 2 November 2026  
**Scope:** Indian automotive equity market and value chain

## Main market context
SIAM's Q2 FY2026–27 release reported domestic passenger-vehicle sales of about 1.36 million units, up 30.7% year-on-year; utility vehicles were 68% of PV sales and grew 34.2%. Commercial-vehicle sales rose 28.7%. SIAM also reported PV exports down 5.2% and flagged West Asia-related logistics constraints and a higher base for H2 comparisons. See [SIAM Q2 FY27 release](https://www.siam.in/pressrelease-details.aspx?mpgid=53&pgidtrail=50&pid=622).

PM E-DRIVE has a stated outlay of ₹11,900 crore and is extended through 31 March 2028 for most supported categories, with category-specific exceptions. See [PM E-DRIVE scheme information](https://pmedrive.heavyindustries.gov.in/about-us). The Government notified new CAFE requirements from 1 April 2027 through 31 March 2032, with an approximately 16.7% tightening in the benchmark across the period. See [PIB CAFE notice](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2316792&lang=2&reg=48).

IEA reports Indian electric-car sales grew 75% in 2025 to 165,000, still close to 4% of total car sales. See [IEA Global EV Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/trends-in-electric-cars).

## Investor approach
The report recommends selective, staged exposure rather than a one-way bullish or bearish view. Monitor segment retail and wholesale demand, dealer inventory, realised prices, margins, cash conversion, supplier lead times, exports, commodity and energy costs, PM E-DRIVE/CAFE notifications, EV warranty economics and valuation. Diversify across business models, require a margin of safety, and size positions for downside.
