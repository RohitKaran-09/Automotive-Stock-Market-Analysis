## Interpretation

M&M outperformed on FY26 sales growth, PBT and PAT growth, reported operating and net margins, current/quick liquidity and calculated returns on average equity/assets. Its operating cash flow also increased strongly. Maruti remains financially conservative on bank borrowing and shows stronger inventory/receivables turnover, but its reported margins compressed and inventory turnover declined in FY26. The interest-coverage values are company-reported and should not be compared mechanically without aligning definitions.

M&M is not a pure-play passenger-vehicle company: the standalone statements include automotive, farm-equipment and investment-related activity. The consolidated group additionally includes financial services and is intentionally not used for this like-for-like comparison. Ratios should be read as a system, with attention to business mix, cash quality, capital intensity and cycle conditions.
