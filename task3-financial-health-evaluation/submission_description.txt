This Task 3 report evaluates the financial health of two publicly listed Indian automotive companies, Mahindra & Mahindra Limited (M&M) and Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL), using their FY2025–26 published financial disclosures and FY2024–25 comparative figures. The objective is to assess each company's profitability, liquidity, solvency, operating efficiency and cash-generation capacity, then identify the strengths, vulnerabilities and relative financial resilience of both businesses.

To improve comparability, the analysis uses standalone financial statements rather than consolidated group results. This is important because M&M's consolidated statements include a large financial-services operation and multiple diversified businesses, which would make direct comparison with Maruti's vehicle-manufacturing operations less meaningful. The report nevertheless explains that M&M standalone includes both automotive and farm-equipment activity and investment income, while Maruti's FY25 comparative figures were restated following the amalgamation of Suzuki Motor Gujarat Private Limited with effect from 1 April 2025.

The report extracts audited income-statement, balance-sheet and cash-flow information for FY26 and FY25. It examines revenue growth, operating margin, profit after tax, net profit margin, return on equity, current ratio, net working capital, debt-to-equity, interest coverage, operating cash flow, capital expenditure and a cash-flow-less-capex proxy. Company-reported ratios are distinguished from calculations performed directly from the published statements. Formula definitions, units, period comparisons and limitations are provided so the reader can reproduce the assessment.

Visualizations compare revenue and profit, profitability margins, liquidity and working capital, leverage and interest coverage, and operating cash flow against capital expenditure. The analysis also considers business context: M&M's mix of SUVs, commercial vehicles, tractors and farm equipment, and Maruti's passenger-car scale, exports, capacity expansion and product mix. Latest interim information available by the 2 November 2026 report cut-off is treated as context rather than mixed into the audited FY26 ratio comparison; notably, M&M's Q2 FY27 results were scheduled for 5 November 2026 and were not yet available at the cut-off.

The conclusion provides a balanced view of the companies' financial resilience, identifies factors requiring monitoring, and explains why no single ratio should be used in isolation. The repository includes the Python script, source data, calculated metrics, charts, Word report and this submission description for reproducibility. The work is educational financial analysis and is not personalized investment advice or a buy/sell recommendation.
